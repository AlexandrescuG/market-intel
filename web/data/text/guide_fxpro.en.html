Registration

  1. Fill in country, email, and password.
  2. On the next screen, enter your name, date of birth, and phone number.

The legal entity isn't named on the registration form

The only line at the bottom of the form reads "FxPro is a registered trademark utilised under an approved licensing agreement." No company, no jurisdiction, no licence number.

  1. To find out who you're actually signing the contract with, open the "Terms and conditions" link on the form → Legal Documentation section.
  2. Open the "Client Agreement" and find clause 1.2 — that is where the entity you are contracting with is named.

Legal entity: FxPro Markets Direct Costa Rica Latam, SRL · Jurisdiction: Costa Rica

«FxPro is the brand and trading name of FxPro Markets Direct Costa Rica Latam, SRL which is a private limited company incorporated in Costa Rica.» (client agreement, clause 1.2)

The registration screen itself doesn't name the entity at all — only the trademark line. You can only find out the real counterparty by opening the PDF agreement, two clicks deep into the documentation, which a regular user isn't required to go through during registration.

Account activation

Where: direct.fxpro.com, dashboard · Screenshots taken: 2026-08-07

  1. Right after registration, a "You are Ready to Start!" screen appears with a QR code for the mobile app.
  2. You land on "Wallet & Accounts": a separate wallet (for moving money between accounts) and a trading account — already created, under the "Real account" tab.

What to notice

The account FxPro creates by default is labelled "Real", not "Demo" — even though the balance is zero and you can't trade until it's funded. If you want to practise without money, switch to the "Demo accounts" tab and create a demo account separately — it isn't selected by default.

What to notice

0% is the fee charged by the FxPro platform itself. It doesn't cancel out your own bank's or payment provider's fees (especially for cards not denominated in USD) — check with your card issuer separately.

Verification

Without verification the account is created and visible in the dashboard, but documents will still be requested before your first withdrawal.

  1. Open the profile menu (icon in the top right corner) → "Settings".
  2. On the "Your documents" tab — status "Identity Verification: Not uploaded". Click "Upload" to attach a document.
  3. There's a separate "Optional documents" block — this is where you upload whatever the platform has asked for beyond standard verification.

Why we don't publish FxPro's spread as a single number

We have three independent measurements of the same EUR/USD, and all three disagree: the live quote on the site's public page — about 1.4 pips; in the same terminal's favourites list — 0.2; in the terminal's full instrument search (below) — 0.1.

A partial explanation turned up right on the instrument page: "Note: Price data indicated above may be delayed by 15 minutes." But a fifteen-minute delay doesn't explain a sevenfold difference between the site and the terminal, so the question isn't fully resolved.

As long as the reason is unknown, "FxPro's spread" doesn't have a single defined value, and we'd rather say that directly than pick whichever number looks best. Only a measurement on a real account, at the actual moment of a trade, resolves it.

  1. To place a trade, click an instrument in the list — a ticket opens with Market / Limit / Stop tabs.
  2. Enter the volume, and if you want, set Stop Loss and Take Profit — both optional, but without a Stop Loss the trade has no limit on losses.

What to notice

"Margin required" under the ticket isn't a fee — it's the amount locked in your account against the open position. It's released when the trade closes, but while the position is open, you can't use it.

Price alerts — without an open position

In the terminal you can set a price alert (the bell icon) — you'll get a notification once the instrument reaches the level you want, without having to keep a trade open or watch the chart.
