Account activation

After registering, open the dashboard — the account already exists. Before trading, fill in a short questionnaire: it unlocks higher limits and is required for verification.

Where: app.naga.com, dashboard · Screenshots taken: 2026-08-07

  1. Click "Get Started" on the "Set Up Your Live Account" prompt — it takes a couple of minutes.
  2. Enter your details exactly as in your passport: name, date of birth, nationality. You'll also be asked separately whether you're a politically exposed person (PEP) and a US tax resident.
  3. Enter your residential address.
  4. A separate questionnaire ("Financial Details") asks about employment, industry, and trading experience, then source of funds, investment goal, income, and total net worth.

The account becomes "real" after the questionnaire

Right after filling it in, the dashboard sends a "You upgraded to a real-money account!" notification — that's not the same thing as identity verification (see the section below), just confirmation that basic data has been accepted and funding methods are available.

"Withdrawable" isn't the same as your balance

The amount available for withdrawal can be less than what's sitting in the account: if you have open positions, you can only withdraw an amount that would keep your margin level above 150% — otherwise the system won't let you submit the request, to protect you from forced position closures.

Verification

Without identity verification, NAGA lets you fund the account up to €2,000 — that figure is stated right on the screen where verification starts, before the process even begins. The check itself is performed by a third-party service, Sumsub, not by NAGA directly.

Where: app.naga.com, dashboard · Sumsub · Screenshots taken: 2026-08-07

  1. Click "Agree and continue" on the consent screen — it explains that Sumsub processes the data, and gives links to the privacy policy.
  2. An optional step: you can create a Sumsub ID so you don't have to verify again on other services that also use Sumsub.
  3. Step 1 of 2: choose the country that issued your document and its type — passport, ID card, or driving licence.
  4. Before uploading — three short requirements: a colour photo, good lighting, no edited images.
  5. Attach a file (JPG, PNG, HEIC, WEBP, or PDF, up to 50 MB) or photograph the document right here.
  6. Step 2 of 2 — a liveness check: the system captures your face and background through the camera to confirm the document's owner is the one submitting it.

If the camera won't turn on

Sumsub shows instructions right on the screen: open the site icon in the browser's address bar → find "Camera" → enable access → come back and click "Try again". In automated or isolated browser environments (for example, virtual machines without a physical camera), camera access is technically unavailable — in that case, "Continue on phone" is the only option left.

Trading

Where: app.naga.com, web terminal · Screenshots taken: 2026-08-07

  1. In "Markets", instruments are grouped by category — indices, forex, commodities, crypto — with a live price and the day's change.
  2. "Positions" shows open, pending, and closed trades — before your first trade, it's empty, with a "Start Trading" button.
  3. Open an instrument — a ticket appears on the right: Sell/Buy, market or specific price, volume in lots or an amount in dollars.

Margin level is explained right in the interface

Formula: (Equity ÷ Margin used) × 100. Below 50%, NAGA starts force-closing positions — starting with the most unprofitable one, until there's enough margin. Between 51% and 150% — a warning and a recommendation to fund the account or close some positions. Above 150% — everything's fine.

A note on symbols

In the terminal, instruments carry a _Pro suffix: EURUSD_Pro, XAUUSD_Pro. And the DAX 40 index is labelled GER30 (DAX) — the ticker is ten stocks behind the index.

Ready-made signals from Trading Central

The "Trading Signals" section has ideas from Trading Central (the same third-party provider AvaTrade uses): instrument, direction, target levels, and a short plain-language scenario.

The base tier gets 5 signals a day — seeing more requires Silver Trader status. Just as with AvaTrade, this isn't our recommendation: the signals are generated automatically by Trading Central's own methodology and are equally available to all clients at the relevant tier.

Built-in copy trading with a public leaderboard

"Find Leaders" shows a real trader ranking by profit over the past 30 and 7 days — with follower and copier counts for each. The "Autocopy" button connects you to automatically copy a specific trader's trades.

This isn't a guarantee of results: a leader's past performance promises nothing about the future, and the profit shown is the leader's own profit, not a guaranteed profit for the copier (fees and copy delay reduce it).

You can also earn from your own followers

"Copy Premium" in the "Wallet" section pays the trader being copied — up to $0.90 per lot on popular assets at the Diamond tier. Conditions: be at least Iron tier, trade your own account (not through copying), hold the trade longer than 5 minutes, invest more than $100 in it, and close it in profit. The tier depends on your account balance — starting from zero, it only becomes available after a deposit of $250 or more.
